[4] antithetic variates, on a payoff with a jump discontinuity

    matched on payoff evaluations: 524288 antithetic pairs vs 1048576 plain

    m | var/eval plain | var/eval antithetic | efficiency
------+----------------+---------------------+-----------
   25 |        74.1716 |             52.8460 |      1.40x   (rho = -0.288)
   50 |        73.0697 |             53.2534 |      1.37x   (rho = -0.271)
  100 |        72.1130 |             53.4349 |      1.35x   (rho = -0.259)

    Efficiency is 1/(1+rho) for correlation rho between a path and its
    reflection. The gain is real but modest: the terminal payoff is
    monotone in the driving normals, which is the case antithetic
    sampling suits, but the knock-out indicator is not. A path and its
    reflection are not both near the barrier, so the negative correlation
    that pays for the second evaluation is much weaker than it would be
    for a vanilla.
